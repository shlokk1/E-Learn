How Doubt Resolution Works

A simple, effective, and moderated workflow to ensure you receive the best academic support.

1

Student Asks Doubt

Submit your question along with the subject, topic, and any supporting images or references.

2

Teacher Reviews

To maintain quality, a teacher reviews the doubt to ensure it is academic and appropriate.

3

Mentor Answers

Once approved, an experienced mentor provides a detailed explanation to your query.

4

Student Learns

Review the answer, ask follow-up questions if needed, and mark the doubt as resolved.
