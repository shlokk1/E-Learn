Hello! That's a great question. When solving for x, we use the reverse of PEMDAS (Order of Operations) which is SADMEP. We want to undo addition and subtraction before we undo multiplication and division.

If you divided by 2 first, you would have to divide EVERY term by 2, making it: x + 2.5 = 7.5. Subtracting 5 first keeps the numbers cleaner: 2x = 10.
